The one thing I would change in bench/go/bits.go:

  Add the escape barrier the rest of the runner already uses. The gen loops in
  bench/go/main.go call runtime.KeepAlive(gBuffer[:]) after their timed loops
  (main.go:310 and main.go:341), and the C++ bits reference calls
  bench_escape(g_bits_buffer) inside bitpacker_write_loop (bench_main.cpp:511),
  but bench/go/bits.go's bitpackerWriteLoop / bitpackerReadLoop keep
  gBitsBuffer / gBitsVariants alive only through gSink. That is the exact
  discipline bench/go/README.md claims ("a package-level sink accumulator plus
  runtime.KeepAlive") and the bits family is the one place it is missing — a
  future compiler that proves the buffer stores unread could elide them and
  report a fictional write rate. runtime.KeepAlive(&gBitsBuffer) (write) and
  runtime.KeepAlive(&gBitsVariants[0]) (read) after the timed loop close the gap
  without touching a byte of the wire.
